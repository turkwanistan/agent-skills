# Phrasebook

Use these as characteristic patterns, not mandatory canned phrases. Choose only wording that fits the facts and communication type.

## Opening a Response

- `Hi [Name],`
- `Hi Team,`
- `Understood.`
- `That sounds good.`
- `Thanks for the heads up.`
- `Thanks for the bump.`
- `Okay, I wasn't sure...`

## Providing an Update

- `It's scheduled.`
- `It's enabled, you can proceed.`
- `It's complete, here's the update...`
- `I shut it down at [TIME].`
- `I noted this on the product ticket and increased the priority.`
- `I've added an internal escalation flag to the ticket...`
- `I'll keep an eye on it.`
- `We'll take a deeper look.`

## Describing Evidence

- `Looking at [EVIDENCE]...`
- `From our investigation...`
- `The chart below shows...`
- `We also identified that...`
- `Here's what I did for transparency:`
- `For reference...`
- `That means about ~[VALUE]...`
- `I included [METRIC] because...`

## Expressing Uncertainty

- `I believe...`
- `I think...`
- `My understanding is...`
- `I'm not 100% sure, but...`
- `I don't know [X]...`
- `it seems to be...`
- `it appears to be...`
- `most likely...`
- `I cannot confirm from my end definitively.`
- `I'm not aware of any conflicts, but...`

## Presenting a Hypothesis

- `My best guess is [CAUSE].`
- `It seems to be failing [CHECK/DEPENDENCY].`
- `The [FAILURE] appears to be caused by [CAUSE].`
- `Could it be something that... ?`
- `I think there was [ACTIVITY], but I cannot confirm...`
- `This has nothing to do with [A]; it appears related to [B].`

## Asking for Information

- `Can you also check [SPECIFIC THING]?`
- `Could you clarify [IMPACT/USE]?`
- `Can you elaborate on what "[TERM]" means?`
- `Do you have specific questions you are looking to have answered?`
- `Could you provide [DATE/TIME/TIMESTAMP]?`
- `Do you have a date in mind, maybe [OPTION A] or [OPTION B]?`
- `Would you be able to share an architecture diagram or a high-level overview?`
- `Please let me know what works for you.`

## Giving Troubleshooting Steps

- `I would say our next best attempt would be to [TEST].`
- `Could you try [ACTION] to see if that resolves [SYMPTOM]?`
- `Can you try again, and give me a precise timestamp so I can associate it with error logging?`
- `Once we get a clean baseline back, [NEXT TEST] can begin.`
- `The path(s) forward would be 1) [A] or 2) [B].`

## Correcting a Misunderstanding

- `I thought the plan was to [X]?`
- `That's correct.`
- `Reading [SOURCE] again, I believe...`
- `I'm curious about your source, but...`
- `Correct me if I'm wrong.`
- `Let me know if you disagree.`

## Explaining Limitations

- `I can only see [METRIC], which includes [LIMITATION], but it is a good estimate otherwise.`
- `I don't know how long [X] will take; [SOURCE] gives us ranges like [RANGE].`
- `I couldn't accurately answer this question from a [LEADERSHIP/OWNER] perspective.`
- `There is no ETA at this point.`
- `From an [APPIAN/OUR] perspective...`
- `[TOOL/PROCESS] is outside our scope or understanding.`

## Setting Expectations

- `Restarts are pretty quick now, like [RANGE].`
- `We usually [PLAN], but the last window took [ACTUAL] for reference.`
- `Once we start [ACTIVITY], [METRIC] will [EXPECTED CHANGE].`
- `We will need to wait until after [MILESTONE] to [ACTION].`
- `If this can wait until [TIME/EVENT], that would be best for [REASON].`

## Recommending a Path

- `I recommend [ACTION].`
- `I think we should [ACTION].`
- `I say we [ACTION].`
- `we should consider [ACTION].`
- `Short-term, [MITIGATION].`
- `Long-term, [DURABLE FIX].`
- `In that case, we're good to [NEXT STEP].`
- `waiting will keep things simple and low-risk.`

## Giving Next Steps

- `Once complete, [RESULT/NEXT STEP].`
- `From there we can [NEXT STEP].`
- `Please monitor the case for any questions/updates from our team.`
- `Please let me know about the workaround, and if it works.`
- `Let me know what I should [DO/CHANGE] back up.`
- `What do you think?`
- `Let me know.`

## Closing

Most characteristic:
- `Thanks,`
- `Regards,`

Less common:
- `Best,`

Very short thread replies may omit a closing entirely.

## Situational Informality

Use sparingly and only with a familiar-contact tone:
- `Nope - ...`
- `Any chance we can sneak in [TEST]?`
- `wiggle room`
- `pretty quick`
- `somewhat secretly`
- `perchance`

These are evidence of looseness, not a set of catchphrases to insert artificially.
